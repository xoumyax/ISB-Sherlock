# W1 plan: near-duplicate collapse and family recount

Status: plan, revised after an independent review; brownie's decisions of 24 Nov applied (§8). Nothing has been run. Measurements are in the appendix.

## 1. Purpose and done-criterion
Prevalence must count clusters, not near-duplicate variants. W1 turns the 315-family upper bound into real family sizes.
**Done (W1 row):** every Tier X executable has a TLSH digest or a recorded reason, and the recount is published. The TLSH half already holds: all 17,865 Tier X executables have a digest.

## 2. Inputs
- **Manifest:** `Manifest.load(resolve_roots())`, columns `sha256 dataset_path pool kind size tlsh mb_family mb_label_source motif_family motif_tier die_packer flags parent_sha256`. Tier X means `mb_label_source == "malwarebazaar_signature"`. The working DB is not read.
- **sdhash:** the bundled binary (`tools.sdhash` in `roots.yaml`), run in bulk mode. Its score threshold S is a placeholder until calibration (§3).

## 3. Method
1. **Candidate pairs, exact:**
   - **Bands:** block by pool. Within a pool, compare only digests whose L-value (`Tlsh.lvalue` from py-tlsh) differs by ≤4.
   - **No hand parsing:** never parse L from the hex. It is stored nibble-swapped, and `int(h[4:6],16)` is wrong on most digests.
   - **Why the band loses nothing:** TLSH's L penalty is 1 for a gap of 1, then 12×gap from 2 on. A gap of 5 therefore costs 60 > 50, while a gap of 4 costs 48 and must stay in.
   - **Function:** use `tlsh.diff`, with length; never `diffxlen`.
   - **Edges kept:** every pair at distance ≤50, as edges.
2. **Graphs:** one per pool for each of executables (pe, dotnet, elf), `other` and `archive`, so non-executables never bridge executables. Thresholds T = 20, 30 and 50 are cut from one edge list, so the clusters nest.
3. **Clusters:** connected components, which is single linkage cut at T. Two bases:
   - **`tlsh`:** TLSH ≤ T.
   - **`confirmed`:** TLSH ≤ T and sdhash ≥ S.
   - **Chaining:** each cluster stores its size and its widest sampled TLSH distance. A cluster wider than 2T is flagged, and W9 propagates only through unflagged confirmed clusters.
4. **sdhash confirmation:**
   - **Digests:** only files that appear in some edge. Lists hold `Manifest.path()` paths, live in `ISB_SCRATCH`, and map back by the `<sha256>.bin` basename.
   - **Calls:** one `-c` call per TLSH-50 component. A component over 2,000 files is processed node by node instead: each node is compared with all of its own TLSH neighbours, in batched query/ref sets, so no edge is lost.
   - **Raw scores:** run at `-t 1` and store raw scores, so S can change without a rerun.
5. **Calibrating S:**
   - **Pairs:** precision is computed over TLSH-30 edges between two Tier X executables. Positives are same-family pairs, negatives different-family pairs.
   - **Random negatives:** a fixed, seeded sample of 20,000 different-family Tier X pairs is sdhash-scored as extra comparisons, which is included in the budget.
   - **Excluded:** cross-pool pairs are findings, not negatives.
   - **Cleaner check:** MOTIF Tier A pairs.
   - **Output:** the curve at S ∈ {1, 5, 10, 16, 21, 30, 40}. One global S: the smallest S reaching precision 0.95, read as a lower bound because aliases count as false links. 21 is the placeholder until calibration runs.
6. **Recount, with one gate (decided):** on the Tier X executables, count clusters per family at **TLSH ≤30, full graph, `tlsh` basis**.
   - **Why this one:** it gives the fewest clusters, so it is the conservative choice for the stop rows: ≥80 families with ≥10 clusters for v0, and 300 families with ≥20 clusters for v1.
   - **Alongside:** T = 20 and 50, the `confirmed` basis, and the induced variant (edges within the family only). Each of these gives the same or more clusters.
   - **Per sample:** `gate_cluster_id` is published at the gating basis, so W3 can date clusters.
7. **Conflict list for W2:** unflagged T = 30 clusters (provisional from `tlsh`, then confirmed; §5) holding two or more `mb_family` values, or an `mb_family` that disagrees with `motif_family`. It is aggregated to family pairs (number of clusters and files, packers, `any_softonic`). Only counts are given at T = 50.

## 4. Special cases
| Case | Handling |
|---|---|
| 85 `oversized_uninventoried` | Singleton with reason `oversized_uninventoried`. No TLSH, no sdhash. None are Tier X. Their TLSH is a separate later sbatch job, off W1's path. |

| `other` | Own graph, outside the executable recount. The 24 Tier X `other` files are listed. |
| `archive` | Own `archive` graph, reported as a finding only, outside the recount and the gate. The bytes are the wrapper; members are clustered as ordinary rows. |
| goodware vs. malware | Collapse within a pool only. A cross-pool edge is never merged; it goes to `cross_pool_near_dups` as a finding. |

## 5. Outputs
- **Data:** `ISB_WORK/enrich/W1/<run_id>/`, as Parquet keyed by sha256 with no absolute paths. Tables in the appendix, plus `recount_report.md`.
- **W2 conflict list, in two steps:**
  - **Provisional:** right after the TLSH stage, built from TLSH-30 clusters (full graph, unflagged) and marked `basis = tlsh_provisional`, so W2 can start.
  - **Confirmed:** once sdhash finishes, built from confirmed, unflagged T = 30 clusters (`basis = confirmed`). It replaces the provisional list.
- **Run records:** `ISB_WORK/runs/W1/<arm>/<run_id>/run.json` via `core.records.RunRecord`, with arms `tlsh_candidates`, `sdhash_confirm` and `cluster_recount`.
- **The working DB is not written by W1.** Tables enter it only after brownie approves a run's outputs. A separate, tested, idempotent loader then writes them to a fresh copy of `manifest.sqlite` (run_id recorded), and brownie swaps that copy in. Until then the outputs are files only, under `enrich/W1/<run_id>/`.

## 6. Checks
- **Unit tests (fakes, written first):**
  - the band is lossless against brute force on digests built by py-tlsh from synthetic bytes;
  - L comes from `Tlsh.lvalue`;
  - components nest across 20, 30 and 50;
  - the width flag fires;
  - no edge crosses pools;
  - the node-by-node path for large components finds every edge;
  - the sdhash output parser handles the extra columns and a missing line;
  - no absolute path reaches an output or `run.json`.
- **Real data (`needs_data`):**
  - every Tier X executable has a TLSH or a reason;
  - every row appears exactly once per (graph, threshold, basis);
  - the 264 identical-digest groups each sit in one cluster at T = 20;
  - a brute-force distance check on 10k random pairs inside the band;
  - 50 conflicts are spot-checked by hand.

## 7. Runtime
- **TLSH:** about 8.4e8 banded diffs at 0.69 µs measured, about 10 core-minutes, or about 30 with Python overhead. An sbatch job with 16 CPUs, 32 GB and 1 h covers it.
- **sdhash:** not measured. It reads at most 269 GB and is likely limited by I/O. It starts with a timed pilot on about 2,000 files and reserves 32 CPUs, 64 GB and 8 h until the pilot says otherwise.
- **Login node:** unit tests only. brownie submits every job.

## 8. Decisions (brownie, 24 Nov)
Recorded in `DECISIONS.md` (rows 1–4 and 8f) and applied above:
1. **Gate:** TLSH ≤30, full graph, `tlsh` basis. The confirmed and induced variants are reported alongside.
2. **sdhash S:** one global S at a precision target of 0.95, read as a lower bound. 21 stays the placeholder until calibration runs.
3. **The 85 oversized files:** singletons for now. Their TLSH is a later, separate sbatch job.
4. **Archives:** own graph, reported as a finding only, outside the recount and the gate.
5. **DB writes:** only after brownie approves a run's outputs, through the loader in §5.
6. **Slip note:** W2 starts Nov 30 and W1 ends Dec 2. A provisional W2 conflict list (`tlsh_provisional`) ships right after the TLSH stage, so W2 can start. The confirmed list replaces it once sdhash finishes, ahead of the full recount report.

- **Tier X:** 17,865 executables in 318 families. 315 have ≥20 files, 3 have <10, and the largest are capped at 200.
- **Identical digests:** 566 rows share a digest, in 264 groups, a surplus of 302.
- **Parents:** 13,860 rows have `parent_sha256`.
- **L-value:** it spans 9–148, so there is no mod-256 wrap. The nibble-swapped parse matched `Tlsh.lvalue` on 20,000 of 20,000 sampled digests, the naive parse on 1,266.
- **Penalty check:** changing only L on a real digest gives 1, 24, 36, 48 and 60 for gaps of 1 to 5. The reviewer measured minima over real pairs of 55 at a gap of 4 and 61 at a gap of 5.
- **Banded pairs (|ΔL| ≤ 4):** malware 5.5e8 of 4.5e9; goodware 3.6e7 of 2.5e8; cross-pool 2.5e8.
- **sdhash input:** 172 GB malware and 97 GB goodware (63 GB Tier X). 3,399 TLSH files are under 512 B.
