# W3 plan: source dates (first-seen dates for drift)

Draft for brownie, 23 Nov 2026 (revised after independent review; updated 24 Nov to brownie's decisions, §8). Plan only; nothing has run. W3 was due 23–25 Nov. It fits only if approved by 24 Nov; any slip goes in the session note.

## 1. Purpose and done-criterion
gen/held and the drift replay (E7) need sample dates from sources, never from inventory time (H6). **Done:** 100% of Tier X has a source date, and the windows file lists samples and families per window. W3 meets this in its own output, `enrich/W3/<run_id>/drift_windows.json`; G1 copies that file into `splits/` with a `.sha256`. Tier X means CSV `mb_label_source == "malwarebazaar_signature"` (17,889 rows). All 17,889 already carry `mb_first_seen`, so the work is the evidence trail, the dates outside Tier X, goodware and the windows.

## 2. Inputs (counts in the appendix)
- **MalwareBazaar:** the run-5 `bazaar_full.csv` dump (`first_seen_utc`; the newest snapshot; runs 1–3 hold older snapshots), the run 1–5 `dataset_manifest.csv` files and `bazaar_report_linked/targeted_manifest.csv`. That last file is the only per-run source for 20 Tier X rows and for 18 non-Tier-X rows.
- **MOTIF:** `report_date` from `labels.extra` and `reports/motif_report_index_matches.csv`. The MOTIF jsonl (`appeared`) was not migrated.
- **Goodware:** `goodware_meta` (os_build, versioninfo, goodware_expand package/version/suite) and the suite `Release` files under `project_state/.../goodware_expansion/2026-10-23/metadata/`. No release date is stored for any goodware file.
- **Access:** only through `resolve_roots`, `Manifest` and `ManifestDB.open` (immutable); exports are streamed. Barred and never read: `files.first_seen_local`, `file_paths.mtime`, `archive_extractions.extracted_at` (inventory times) and `pe_meta.timestamp` (compile time, forgeable).

## 3. Method
- **Tier X:** `date` = MB `first_seen_utc`. This puts all Tier X on one clock, so window counts sum to the 17,865 Tier X executables. The dump must equal `mb_first_seen` and the per-run export; any mismatch fails the run (0 today).
- **MOTIF:** report dates are always kept as evidence columns. They are the `date` only for non-Tier-X MOTIF rows that have no MB date (day precision; for multiple dates use the earliest, and record all of them).
- **Other malware:** MB dump date if present; otherwise none, flagged `undated`. The 77,441 undated rows are excluded from the drift windows and from gen/held persistence ordering. They stay usable where no date is needed (FP background, promiscuity). They get no inherited cluster dates.
- **Disagreement:** flag `sources_disagree` when MB and MOTIF differ by more than 30 days. The flag never changes `date`.
- **Goodware:** OS-build constant (win8 = 26 Oct 2012, general availability), then suite `Release` date (an upper bound), then the UPX parent's date. Never acquisition or inventory time; winget stays undated until W7. ubuntu_2610's Release date is 8 days before collection; it counts as an upper bound with low confidence.
- **Timezone:** MB strings are naive (`2020-05-04 21:55:29`). UTC is attached because the dump documents `first_seen_utc`. Each source declares its tz in config; a naive value from a source with no declared tz is rejected. Precision ∈ {second, day, suite, os_build}.
- **Plausibility:** no date before 1995 (PE era); none after the source's snapshot (the MB dump's "Last updated", the MOTIF audit time); no MB date before the dump's first row. UPX children equal their parent by construction. An archive's date is an upper bound on its members, so a later member date is flagged, not failed. No dated row has a parent today.
- **W1 clusters:** dates stay per sample. `cluster_date` = earliest member date over W1's `gate_cluster_id` (TLSH ≤30, full graph, tlsh basis), plus a count of undated members. A cluster date never passes to an undated member.

## 4. drift_windows.json
- **Scheme:** calendar quarters in UTC, 2020Q1 to `2026Q3+` (E7). 2020Q1 is thin (92 samples). 2026Q4 is partial (34 samples; the dump ends 17 Oct), so it is merged into 2026Q3 as `2026Q3+` (1,231 samples). Executables only: the 24 Tier X `other` rows are left out of the windows and listed separately in `coverage.json`.
- **Collection bias:** the MB runs pulled the recent feed. 2026Q3 alone holds 1,197 samples but only 70 families. E7 should read per-quarter volume as an artefact of collection, not of prevalence.
- **Per window:** `id`, `start`/`end` (half-open UTC); Tier X sha256s; families (`mb_family` before alias merges) with counts; `n_undated_excluded`; dated goodware with precision; `source_counts`. There are no split fields; the split is G1's, built from scratch. The file is regenerated after W1/W2.
- **Goodware coverage is almost nil inside the windows.** win8 (2012) predates every window. The Linux suite dates are all 2026 upper bounds. 93 of the 100 UPX parents are undated (only the 7 win8 parents carry a date). Everything else is undated. How E7 measures FP drift is decided at E7 (§8); W3 records the goodware dates it can. The 12 goodware hashes found in the MB dump carry `mb_goodware_hit`. They go to pool review and stay out of G_stats and G_eval until that is decided.

## 5. Outputs (ISB_WORK/enrich/W3/<run_id>/; Parquet; sha256 keys; no absolute paths)
- `sample_dates.parquet`: sha256, pool, date (UTC), precision, source, mb_first_seen_utc, motif_report_dates, evidence (JSON: every source date with file and row), confidence, flags (`undated`, `sources_disagree`, `upper_bound`, `inherited_parent`, `mb_goodware_hit`), run_id. It is the `sample_dates` manifest table. It enters the DB only after brownie approves this run's outputs. A separate, tested, idempotent loader then writes it to a fresh copy of `manifest.sqlite`, recording the run_id, and brownie swaps that copy in. Until then the table exists only as files.
- `cluster_dates.parquet` (after W1), `drift_windows.json`, `coverage.json` (done-criterion result and appendix counts), `conflicts.parquet`.
- `run.json` through `RunRecord(experiment="W3", arm="source_dates")` at `ISB_WORK/runs/W3/source_dates/<run_id>/run.json`: commit, config, root sources, input sha256s, drops by reason.

## 6. Checks and tests
- **Unit (fakes):** a barred column raises; Tier X takes MB even when MOTIF is earlier; MOTIF m/d/Y parsing; naive input without a declared tz is rejected; plausibility bounds; quarter edges; UPX inheritance; undated and `other` rows never reach a window; no split keys; no absolute path in outputs.
- **Real data:** 17,889/17,889 Tier X dated; window counts sum to 17,865 (plus 24 `other` listed separately); every appendix count is reproduced in `coverage.json`.

## 7. Runtime
Minutes on a login node, no sbatch. Basis: in this plan's measurements the CSV, the 1.16M-row dump and the MOTIF index scanned in 4.6 s; the DB fetches took seconds. No sample bytes are read.

## 8. Decisions (brownie, 24 Nov)
The full record is in `DECISIONS.md`, rows 5–8f.
1. **Precedence:** Tier X uses MB `first_seen_utc`. MOTIF dates are kept as evidence with a disagreement flag, and are the date only for non-Tier-X MOTIF rows. Earliest-wins was rejected: it would have moved 11 of the 26 Tier X rows also in MOTIF, 7 of them to another quarter and 1 to before 2020Q1.
2. **Undated malware (77,441 rows):** excluded from the windows and from gen/held ordering, and flagged `undated`. They stay usable where no date is needed. No inherited cluster dates.
3. **FP drift:** decided when E7 is designed; the gap is recorded. The options on file: a fixed G_eval scored per window; dated goodware via W5–W7; Authenticode countersignature times, with chain checks.
4. **win8:** a constant of 26 Oct 2012 (GA). **winget:** left undated; revisit with W7.
5. **12 goodware hashes in the MB dump:** sent to pool review, and kept out of G_stats and G_eval until decided.
6. **2026Q4:** merged into 2026Q3 as `2026Q3+`. **24 Tier X `other` rows:** out of the windows (executables only), listed separately.
7. **DB writes:** only after brownie approves a run's outputs, through the separate idempotent loader into a fresh copy that brownie swaps in.
